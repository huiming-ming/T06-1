Data Sources and Notes

Source: Ex6_TVdata.csv, supplied with the COS30045 T06 exercise materials. The supplied materials identify the dataset as downloaded in January 2025.

Annual energy consumption is measured in kWh/year. Screen size is measured in inches. Frequency counts CSV records, not sales or households.

Histogram intervals include their lower boundary and exclude their upper boundary, except the final interval, which includes both boundaries.

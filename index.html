Data Sources and Notes

Source: Ex6_TVdata.csv, supplied with the COS30045 T06 starter pack. The supplied materials identify the data as downloaded in January 2025.

Energy consumption is measured in kWh/year. Frequency counts CSV records, not sales or households. Intervals include their lower boundary and exclude their upper boundary, except the final interval, which includes both.
